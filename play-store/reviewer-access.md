# Play reviewer access

Create a permanent reviewer company after production deployment. Do not use an owner account that could delete production data.

```text
Login email: REVIEWER_EMAIL
Password: REVIEWER_PASSWORD

Instructions:
1. Sign in with the credentials above.
2. The account opens a sample company containing representative available and checked-out tools.
3. Open Tools to browse inventory and Tool details to view QR and history.
4. Open Scan QR to test camera permission. A printable sample QR should be attached to the review notes if physical scanning is impractical.
5. No subscription, invitation, location restriction, or one-time code is required.
```

Replace the placeholders with active credentials immediately before submission and verify them on a clean device.
